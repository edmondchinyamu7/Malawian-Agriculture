MAFUNSO OFUNSIDWA KAWIRIKAWIRI • FAQ

Mafunso Okhudza Umembala wa Ulimi wa Malawi

❓ Kodi ndimalipira bwanji ndi Airtel Money kapena TNM Mpamba?

Mukasankha plan imene mukufuna, dinani batani la planiyo. Paneli ya malipiro idzatseguka ndi nambala ya malonda (Merchant code: 548921 pa Airtel kapena 883100 pa TNM). Tumizani ndalama ndipo lembani nambala ya chitupa (SMS reference) kuti mutsegule umembala nthawi yomweyo.

❓ Kodi ndingathe kugwiritsa ntchito mapulaniwa ngati ndilibe intaneti m'munda?

Inde! Alimi a pa Professional ndi Agribusiness amatha kutsitsa mabuku onse a m'munda (PDF) ndipo zolemba zonse zomwe mwasunga zimakhalabe pa foni yanu ngakhale mulibe intaneti.

❓ Kodi ndingasinthe kapena kuleka plan nthawi ina iliyonse?

Mutha kukweza kapena kusintha plan nthawi iliyonse. Palibe ndalama zowonjezera kapena mikangano; umembala wanu umagwira ntchito mpaka tsiku loyenera lisanathe.

❓ Kodi ndingatsegule bwanji zida ngati Kalendala kapena Chowerengera Feteleza?

Zida zonse zili ndi loko ya umembala. Mukakhala pa Standard, Kalendala ya ulimi ndi Malo ophunzirira zimatseguka; mukakhala pa Professional, Chowerengera Feteleza ndi Dongosolo la Munda zimatseguka zonse basi.
