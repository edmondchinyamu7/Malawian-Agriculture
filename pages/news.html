DARS Releases Two Drought-Tolerant Hybrid Maize Varieties

Developed at Chitedze Agricultural Research Station. Varieties mature in 115-125 days and produce flint grain suitable for local pounding (nsima) with low cob rotting under mid-season dry spells.
