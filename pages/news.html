Affordable Inputs Programme (AIP) Distribution Schedule

Redemption centres designated in Lilongwe, Kasungu, Mzimba, and Shire Valley. Farmers are advised to present their National ID cards and confirm seed varieties before leaving retail sheds.

Ministry of Agriculture • Official Release
🔬

DARS Releases Two Drought-Tolerant Hybrid Maize Varieties

Developed at Chitedze Agricultural Research Station. Varieties mature in 115-125 days and produce flint grain suitable for local pounding (nsima) with low cob rotting under mid-season dry spells.

Research & Extension Advisory
🌍

ADMARC Depots Commence Legume & Grain Purchasing

Minimum farmgate floor prices implemented across regional depots to protect smallholders from predatory middleman price undercutting.
