Malawi Soil Information Centre

Understand the physical and chemical characteristics of soils in your district. Learn how to address soil acidity, prevent erosion, and build enduring organic matter.

🏜️

Ferralsols & Luvisols (Red / Sandy Loams)

Locations: Common across Lilongwe plain, Kasungu, and Mzimba plateau.

Characteristics: Deep, well-drained, easy to cultivate, but frequently depleted of organic nitrogen and available phosphorus.

Management: Incorporate cattle or poultry manure, rotate with legumes (soya, groundnuts), and practice conservation agriculture.

🌑

Vertisols / Makande (Heavy Black Clay Soils)

Locations: Prevalent in the Lower Shire Valley (Chikwawa and Nsanje) and parts of Phalombe plain.

Characteristics: Highly fertile with strong nutrient-holding capacity, but sticky when wet and cracks deeply when dry.

Management: Practice early ridge preparation before soil dries hard. Ideal for cotton, sorghum, and flood-recession crops.

🌊

Fluvisols / Alluvial Soils (Dambo / Riverine)

Locations: Lakeside plains (Salima, Nkhotakota, Karonga) and along major river drainage networks.

Characteristics: High silt and clay content, good moisture retention into dry seasons.

Management: Well-suited to flooded rice production in summer and irrigated horticultural dimba crops in winter.

🧪

Soil Acidity & Agricultural Lime (Malawi Calcitic / Dolomitic)

The Challenge: Continuous usage of acidifying fertilisers without organic inputs lowers pH below 5.5, locking up phosphorus.

Solution: Apply agricultural limestone at 1 to 2 tonnes/ha based on soil tests from Chitedze or Bvumbwe Agricultural Research Stations.
