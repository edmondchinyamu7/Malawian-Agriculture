The 4R Nutrient Stewardship Principle

Ensure highest nutrient use efficiency and prevent input wastage on your farm:

  • Right Source: Match fertiliser blend to crop and soil needs.
  • Right Rate: Do not under-apply or over-apply nutrients.
  • Right Time: Apply when crops actively demand food (basal vs vegetative peak).
  • Right Place: Place fertilizer 5-10cm from plant base and cover with soil.
🧪

Soil Testing & Manure Blending

Combining well-composted farmyard manure with inorganic fertiliser reduces total fertiliser expenditure by up to 30% while revitalising beneficial soil microbes.
