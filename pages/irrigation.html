V12 • IRRIGATION
Water-Smart Agriculture

Irrigation and Dry-Season Farming Guidance

Maximize year-round farm productivity. Move beyond sole reliance on rainfed seasons by adopting cost-effective water harvesting and irrigation technologies.

☀️
Renewable Energy

Solar-Powered Pumping

Low Operational Cost

Photovoltaic pump sets drawing from boreholes, shallow wells, or perennial streams directly into elevated holding tanks. Eliminates costly diesel and petrol fuel expenses.

💧
High Efficiency

Drip Irrigation Kits

Targeted Root Delivery

Delivers water drop-by-drop directly to plant bases. Reduces water loss from evaporation by up to 60% compared to furrow flooding and suppresses weed emergence between beds.

🌾
Traditional & Effective

Dambo & Residual Moisture (Dimba)

Wetland Winter Farming

Cultivating high-value green maize, tomatoes, cabbage, and beans in wetlands during dry months using natural water table capillary action and shallow trenches.

Catchment Protection Guideline: Maintain vegetative riverbank buffer zones of at least 10 meters from stream edges to prevent siltation, collapse of river channels, and degradation of community water tables.
