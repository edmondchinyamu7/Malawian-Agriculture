2. Information the platform may process

English: Depending on the features you use, information may include:

  • Account details such as first name, surname and email address supplied during registration.
  • Account profile fields used by the application, including first name, last name and account role.
  • Basic service activity, such as an application-open event recorded when an authenticated user opens the app.
  • Saved learning topics stored in your browser’s local storage on that device.
  • Technical information that hosting, authentication or other service providers may process to deliver and protect the website, such as device, browser, network and diagnostic information.

Chichewa: Kutengera ndi ntchito zomwe mumagwiritsa ntchito, tsambali lingagwiritse ntchito dzina lanu loyamba, surname, imelo, zambiri za akaunti, mbiri ya kagwiritsidwe ntchito ka pulogalamuyi, mitu yomwe mwasunga pa msakatuli wa foni kapena kompyuta yanu, komanso zambiri zaukadaulo zomwe zimathandiza kuyendetsa ndi kuteteza tsambali.

3. Why information is used

English: Information is used to create and manage accounts, authenticate users, display account features, remember locally saved topics, maintain service reliability, diagnose errors, protect the platform and respond to legitimate requests. We should only use personal information for clear purposes and an applicable lawful basis.

Chichewa: Zambiri zimagwiritsidwa ntchito popanga ndi kusamalira maakaunti, kutsimikizira olowa, kuonetsa ntchito za akaunti, kusunga mitu pa chipangizo chanu, kukonza zolakwika, kuteteza tsambali komanso kuyankha zopempha zoyenera. Zambiri za munthu ziyenera kugwiritsidwa ntchito pa zolinga zomveka komanso zovomerezeka ndi malamulo.

These providers may process technical information as part of hosting, authentication, security and service delivery, subject to their own terms and privacy documentation.

Chichewa: Khodi yomwe tsambali likugwiritsa ntchito panopa imagwiritsa ntchito Supabase Authentication ndi mbiri ya akaunti ya Supabase. Choncho Supabase imagwiritsa ntchito zambiri zofunika kuti akaunti igwire ntchito. Tsambali limasungidwa pa Vercel. Makampaniwa angagwiritse ntchito zambiri zaukadaulo poyendetsa, kutsimikizira ndi kuteteza tsambali, motsatira mfundo zawo zachinsinsi.

5. Browser storage, cookies and analytics

English: The current bookmark feature stores saved topic identifiers in your browser using local storage under the key “ulimiSaved”. This is stored on your device and is not the same as a server-side account record. Your browser and service providers may also use cookies or similar technologies for essential operation, security or preferences. The operator should confirm any additional analytics or tracking tools before publication.

Chichewa: Ntchito yosungira mitu imagwiritsa ntchito local storage ya msakatuli wanu, pansi pa dzina loti “ulimiSaved”. Zimenezi zimasungidwa pa chipangizo chanu ndipo si zofanana ndi mbiri yosungidwa pa seva. Msakatuli kapena makampani othandizira angagwiritsenso ntchito cookies pa ntchito zofunika, chitetezo kapena kukumbukira zokonda. Woyendetsa tsambali ayenera kutsimikizira ngati pali zida zina zotsatira kagwiritsidwe ntchito asanatulutse mfundozi.

English: The weather feature identifies Open-Meteo as a weather-data source. When you request weather information, the selected location or district may be sent to that provider to return a forecast. Links to external websites are governed by those websites’ own privacy policies. We do not control their practices.

Chichewa: Gawo la zanyengo limatchula Open-Meteo ngati gwero la zambiri za nyengo. Mukapempha zanyengo, malo kapena boma lomwe mwasankha lingatumizidwe kwa woperekayo kuti abweze zolosera. Maulalo opita ku masamba ena amatsatira mfundo zachinsinsi za masambawo. Ife sitilamulira mmene amagwiritsira ntchito zambiri.
