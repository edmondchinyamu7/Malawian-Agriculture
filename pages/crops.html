Laibulale ya Mbewu

Mbewu Zotchuka za m'Malawi (Makadi a Mbewu)

Malangizo a kalimidwe katsatanetsatane oyenera nyengo ndi nthaka zosiyanasiyana m'Malawi. Dziwani kagwiritsidwe ntchito, zoyenera kutsatira m'munda, feteleza woyenera, ndi tizilombo tosokoneza mbewu.

Mbewu 16
Chidziwitso pa mbewu: Malangizowa ndi othandiza poyambira kuphunzira. Kusankha mtundu wa mbewu, nthawi yobzala, mipata, ndi feteleza (monga 23:10:5+6S ndi Urea) zimatengera boma, nthaka, ndi mvula. Tsimikizirani ndi mlangizi wazaulimi wa m'dera lanu (AEDO) kapena ku ADD.
