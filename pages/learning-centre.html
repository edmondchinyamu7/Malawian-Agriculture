Knowledge Building

Farmer Academy & Self-Paced Learning

Curated agricultural modules designed for learners, commercializing smallholders, and agricultural vocational students across Malawi.

📚

Course 1: Seed Selection & Germination Auditing

Distinguish genuine certified seed labels (e.g., green/blue security tags) from counterfeit seed bags. Conduct home germination tests with 100 seeds in moist cloth before planting.

🌾

Course 2: Conservation Agriculture (Mthirira & Mulch)

Master minimum soil disturbance, Sasakawa spacing configurations, crop residue retention, and legume intercropping for drought resilience.

💰

Course 3: Farm Enterprise Record Keeping

Track cash outlays on land tilling, certified seeds, fertilisers, chemical sprays, and harvest labour to determine exact breakeven production costs per 50kg bag.

🫙

Course 4: Post-Harvest Loss Abatement

Learn the salt-bottle test for moisture checking (<13%), proper construction of ventilated improved cribs, and proper sealing of Purdue Improved Crop Storage (PICS) bags.
