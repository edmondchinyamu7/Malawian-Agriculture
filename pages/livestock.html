Malawi Livestock Farming Centre

Practical guides for managing dual-purpose poultry, local and Boer goat crossbreeding, smallholder dairy cows, and integrated manure cycling.

🐔
Dual Purpose Poultry

Kuweta Nkhuku (Kuroiler / Black Australorp)

Eggs & Meat Production

Hardy breeds well-adapted to scavenging with supplemental maize bran (gaga) and sunflower cake. Vaccinate regularly against Newcastle disease (Chitopa) using I-2 thermostable vaccine.

🐐
Small Ruminants

Kuweta Mbuzi (Boer Crosses & Small East African)

Raised Slatted Housing

Construct kholas with elevated slatted timber floors (approx. 1 meter high) to allow manure drop-through, preventing foot-rot and worm build-up. Deworm at the beginning and end of rainy seasons.

🐄
Dairy & Beef

Ng'ombe za Mkaka (Dairy Friesian & Crosses)

Zero-Grazing Stalls

Thrive in cooler elevations (Dedza, Bvumbwe, Ntchisi, Mzuzu). Feed high-protein fodder grasses like Napier grass (Bana grass) mixed with Rhodes grass and desmodium legumes.
